support FAQs

Get detailed answers about automated ticketing, real-time tracking, data security, and platform features.

How does AI handle ticket automation?

The AI system reviews, categorizes, and responds to tickets using your organization's knowledge base and historical data, reducing manual intervention and accelerating resolution times.

Can I monitor ticket progress live?

Yes. Access your dashboard anytime to view ticket statuses, updates, and AI-generated responses in real time.

Is my data protected on this platform?

All ticket data is encrypted and access is restricted to authorized users, ensuring confidentiality and compliance with industry standards.

What IT issues are supported?

Submit requests for hardware, software, network, and access problems. The AI agent is trained to address most standard IT support scenarios.

How fast are responses delivered?

Most tickets receive an AI-generated reply within minutes. Complex cases are escalated to IT specialists for further assistance.

Can I adjust AI response settings?

Administrators can customize response templates and update the knowledge base to ensure accurate, company-specific solutions.

Submit your IT support request, ask technical questions, or schedule a demo. Our team will respond promptly with accurate solutions.
